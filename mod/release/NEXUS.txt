NAME: GalleryDance
SUMMARY: Looping dances for all 11 Gallery heroines, picked from an in-game panel (F7) with speed control. Physics stays on.
CATEGORY: Animations   |  ADULT: yes  |  AI-GENERATED: ticked (like BodySlider/FreeCam)
REQUIREMENTS: UE4SS v3.0.1
PERMISSIONS: no Donation Points for this file (Patrik and Ghost Dance terms forbid commercial use)

---- DESCRIPTION (BBCode) ----
[size=5][b]GalleryDance[/b][/size]
Makes the Gallery heroines dance. Open the Gallery, press [b]F7[/b], click a dance, and it loops with hair and breast physics still on.

[b]Dances[/b] (on Blythe, Celestina, DEMI, Elaisha, Fortune, FowChan, Huntress, Killi, Lily, Sova, Taron)
[list]
[*]Twerk
[*]Belly Dance
[*]Bellydancing
[*]Dancing
[*]Addiction
[*]Patrik and The Small Guy
[*]Helltaker
[*]Ghost Dance
[/list]

[b]Panel[/b]
[list]
[*]Click a dance to loop it
[*]SPEED 0.5 / 1 / 1.5 / 2
[*]MIRROR: play every dance left-right flipped
[*]STOP: back to the pose selected in the Gallery menu
[*]F10: hide the panel for screenshots
[*]F7 or CLOSE: hide the panel
[/list]

[b]Requirements[/b]
[url=https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/releases]UE4SS v3.0.1[/url] in Subverse\Subverse\Binaries\Win64

[b]Install[/b]
Extract the zip into the game folder (the one with Subverse.exe), merging folders.
Updating from 1.0: overwrite the files.
Uninstall: delete ue4ss\Mods\GalleryDance and Content\Paks\~mods\GalleryDance_P.pak.

[b]Known issues[/b]
[list]
[*]Patrik ends in a crouch, so there is a small jump when it loops (about every 3 minutes).
[/list]

[b]Compatibility[/b]
Tested on GOG v1.2 with the Celestina DLC. Doesn't change any game files. Pairs well with [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/subverse/mods/25]BodySlider[/url] and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/subverse/mods/26]FreeCam[/url].
